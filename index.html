ThirstDrop – Quench your thirst, save the Earth

Addressing Two Urgent Problems With One Smart Solution: Plastic Waste and Lack of Clean Water.

Our smart water vending machine allows users to exchange empty plastic bottles for clean drinking water. We reward sustainability and promote inclusive hydration for all.

Who We Are

We’re a team of student innovators and environmental advocates with backgrounds in engineering, sustainability, and design. ThirstDrop was born out of a desire to rethink how we handle plastic waste and make hydration both accessible and eco-friendly.

Product Features

  • ♻️ Eco-Powered Hydration: Accepts used plastic bottles, promotes recycling.
  • 💧 Clean Water, Always: Dispenses safe, filtered water for public spaces.
  • ⚙️ User-Friendly Design: Easy interface, accessible for all.
  • 🔋 Smart & Sustainable: Solar-powered with bottle recognition tech.
  • 📊 Track Your Impact: Optional screen showing recycled bottle stats.

How It Works

  1. Deposit a Used Plastic Bottle
  2. Machine Verifies the Bottle
  3. Receive Water Instantly
  4. Repeat or Share the Experience

FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)

  • Q: What types of bottles does ThirstDrop accept?
    A: Standard PET plastic bottles (300ml to 500ml).
  • Q: How much water do I get per bottle?
    A: Typically, 150ml of clean water per bottle.
  • Q: Is the water safe to drink?
    A: Yes. All machines have multi-stage filtration systems.
  • Q: Where can I find ThirstDrop machines?
    A: Currently in selected schools, parks, and communities.
  • Q: Can I suggest a machine location?
    A: Absolutely! Contact us with your ideas.
